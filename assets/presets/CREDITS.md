# Studio light presets

Three-point, Sunrise and Cloudy were adapted from the light collections
credited to Reiner and Draise in the
[Bforartists Default Library](https://github.com/Bforartists/Bforartists/blob/master/scripts/addons_core/bfa_default_library/Default%20Library/Default_Library_01.blend).
The [Bforartists manual](https://www.bforartists.de/data/manualbfa4/28.1_Asset_Browser_-_Default_Asset_LIbrary.pdf)
shows the collections in the Asset Browser.

HoudonRef uses its own light units and shadow model. Three-point uses the
original arrangement and 60° spot cones with shorter distances. Sunrise and
Cloudy use directional lights and adjusted strengths for the default Room.
